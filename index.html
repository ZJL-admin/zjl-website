About us

A focused trading house built on supply-chain discipline.

Founded in Hong Kong in 2018, Zhijinlian focuses on global commodity trading, with non-ferrous metals at its core and rubber and other industrial raw materials alongside.

Our team brings long-standing relationships across the metals supply chain, from leading international trading houses and producers to established industrial buyers in Greater China and Southeast Asia. That network lets us secure reliable, quality-assured supply and place it efficiently with end users.

We work with every partner on clear contract terms, prudent risk controls and timely settlement, and we aim to grow alongside our suppliers, customers and banks on a sound, transparent footing.

How we trade

From origin to end user, managed at every step.

  1. 01

    Source

    We secure material from established international suppliers and producers, verifying origin, brand and specification before commitment.

  2. 02

    Contract

    We agree clear terms with our customers on price, quantity, quality and delivery, aligned with the terms of supply.

  3. 03

    Settle

    Payments are settled by telegraphic transfer or documentary letters of credit through our banking partners.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    We coordinate shipping documents, title transfer and logistics through to final receipt by the customer.
